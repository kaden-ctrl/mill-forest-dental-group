Our practice

Built by neighbors, for neighbors

For more than 30 years, Mill Forest Dental Group has cared for Clear Lake families with calm, honest, personal dentistry.

We built this practice around a simple belief: dentistry should feel calm, honest, and personal. Our team takes time to explain your options in plain language, respects your budget, and greets you and your kids by name. Many of our patients have been with us so long we've watched their families grow, and grow up.

Meet the doctors

Five dentists, one friendly team

Every doctor here lives locally, knows their patients by name, and takes the time to get your care right.

Jen L. Butler, DDS

Loves seeing families grow up with us

“I cannot express enough how much I love my career in dentistry. I feel honored that people choose me to take care of their oral health and make their smiles beautiful. After many years of practice, I have seen so many families grow, and grow up!”

Dr. Butler meets monthly with a study club of general dentists and specialists to review complex cases, and trains yearly through hands-on Spear workshops and the Las Vegas Institute for Advanced Dental Studies. She lives in League City with her husband Dan, an aerospace engineer, and their three boys.

  • BA in Microbiology, University of Texas at Austin
  • DDS, UTHealth Houston School of Dentistry
  • Fellow, Academy of General Dentistry

Jennifer H. Hermstein, DDS

Your neighbor at the store and the soccer game

“I love that our patients share their lives with us. Living in the same community where I practice, I see my patients at the store or at soccer games, and that really makes a difference in building trusting relationships.”

Dr. Hermstein is working toward Mastership in the Academy of General Dentistry, which requires more than double the annual continuing education of any other affiliation. A San Antonio native, she has lived in League City since 2002 with her husband Scott and their three sons.

  • BA, University of Texas at Austin
  • DDS, UT Health Science Center at San Antonio
  • Fellow, Academy of General Dentistry

Samantha J. Tutt, DDS

Grew up in a dental family

“Having grown up with a pediatric dentist for a mother, dentistry seemed like a perfect fit, and it has been. No two patients are alike, and I love the uniqueness each one brings to our office.”

Dr. Tutt is pursuing her Fellowship with the Academy of General Dentistry and volunteers dental care through Give Kids a Smile Day, Lighthouse Ministries, and Texas Mission of Mercy. She and her husband live in La Porte, where they are raising two daughters, because they both love the Gulf Coast.

  • BS in Biology, University of Houston
  • DDS, UTHealth Houston School of Dentistry
  • Member, ADA, TDA, and Greater Houston Dental Society

Andrew Augustine, DDS

Serving the hometown he grew up in

“I found my passion for dentistry at a young age when I lost my permanent front tooth. My dentists helped me recover my smile and my confidence, and I'm excited to do the same for my patients.”

Born in Webster and a Clear Springs High School graduate, Dr. Augustine played collegiate golf at Southwestern University before earning his dental degree in Houston. He is certified in nitrous sedation to help anxious patients relax, and is a member of the Academy of Laser Dentistry.

  • BS in Biochemistry, Southwestern University
  • DDS, UTHealth Houston School of Dentistry
  • Certified in nitrous sedation; member, ALD, TDA, ADA

Kenny Hasson, DDS

Building relationships, one smile at a time

“Dentistry lets me live out my two greatest passions: building strong relationships in my community and caring for the people in it. I strive to create a comfortable environment and be a provider my patients can trust.”

Dr. Hasson grew up in Deer Park and lives right here in the Clear Lake area with his two miniature dachshunds, Finn and Winston. He stays current through continuing education courses, dental journals, and lectures on the latest techniques and technology.

  • BS in Biology, University of Texas at San Antonio
  • DDS, UTHealth Houston School of Dentistry
  • Member, Texas Dental Association and ADA
