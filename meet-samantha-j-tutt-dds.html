Meet the doctors

Samantha J. Tutt, DDS

Meet Samantha J. Tutt, DDS, one of five dentists caring for Clear Lake families at Mill Forest Dental Group.

Grew up in a dental family

“Having grown up with a pediatric dentist for a mother, going into dentistry myself seemed like a perfect fit, and it has been! No two patients are alike, and I love the uniqueness each one brings to our office.”

Dr. Tutt is pursuing her Fellowship with the Academy of General Dentistry, a lifelong commitment to advanced education, and stays current on the latest technology and research through the Spear Study Club. She volunteers dental care through Give Kids a Smile Day, Lighthouse Ministries, and Texas Mission of Mercy.

She and her husband are both natives of La Porte, where they live today because they love the Gulf Coast. They have two beautiful daughters, and beyond dentistry she enjoys gardening, yoga, and traveling.

Education and credentials

  • Bachelor of Science in Biology, University of Houston
  • Doctorate of Dental Surgery, UTHealth Houston School of Dentistry
  • Member, ADA, TDA, Greater Houston Dental Society, and AGD
