Services

Complete care, all under one roof

Preventive, cosmetic, and restorative dentistry for the whole family, so you rarely need a referral elsewhere.

(01)  Preventive and general

Healthy teeth for life

Regular visits every six months keep small problems small, and give you time to talk with your doctor.

Exams and cleanings

Your regular exam takes about 45 minutes. We check for problems you may not see or feel, look for cavities and signs of gum disease, then clean, polish, and rinse your teeth to remove tartar and plaque.

Gum disease care

Gum disease is one of the top causes of tooth loss in adults, and because it is virtually pain-free, many patients never notice it. We measure the space between your teeth and gums at every checkup and treat early, from deep cleanings to laser therapy.

Fluoride and sealants

A fluoride treatment takes just a few minutes and strengthens teeth against decay. Sealants protect the tiny grooves your toothbrush cannot reach, for kids and adults alike.

Nightguards and mouthguards

If you wake with jaw pain or headaches, a custom nightguard prevents the wear of nighttime grinding. Custom sports mouthguards protect your smile in any full-contact sport.

(02)  Cosmetic

An art and a science

Do you hesitate when you smile? Cosmetic dentistry restores confidence with natural-looking results.

Teeth whitening

A safe, effective way to brighten slightly discolored or yellow teeth, for patients in good dental health who want to look their best.

Veneers

Thin, custom-made porcelain shells that cover the front of your teeth to correct gaps, chips, stains, and misshapen teeth. Modeled on your smile and crafted by a dental technician just for you.

Bonding

A conservative, single-visit repair for slightly chipped, discolored, or crooked teeth. Tooth-colored filling material bonds to your tooth and closely matches your natural shade.

(03)  Clear aligners

Straighter teeth, no metal

A system of clear, removable trays that gradually move teeth into position, without wires or brackets.

How they work

Each custom aligner fits over your teeth and moves them a little further. You wear them at least 20 hours a day and switch to a new aligner about every two weeks. Treatment runs from six months to two years depending on your case.

Why patients love them

  • Easier to keep clean, so it is easier to keep gums healthy
  • Comfortable, with no metal to irritate cheeks
  • Removable, so brushing and flossing stay simple
  • Nearly invisible in everyday life
(04)  Restorative

Repair, replace, restore

From a single filling to a full implant, we rebuild smiles that look and feel natural.

Dental implants

A titanium implant takes the place of a missing root, topped with a tooth-colored crown. Properly maintained, implants can last a lifetime, and no one will suspect you have a replacement tooth.

Crowns and bridges

A crown becomes your tooth's new outer surface, strengthening teeth that are broken, worn, or decayed. A bridge replaces missing teeth, maintains the shape of your face, and relieves stress on your bite.

Tooth-colored fillings

Composite resins mimic the appearance of natural teeth, on front teeth where appearance matters and on back teeth alike.

Root canals

When a cracked tooth or deep cavity lets infection inside, root canal treatment clears it and saves the tooth, usually in one to three comfortable visits. Restored teeth can last a lifetime with normal care.

Dentures and extractions

Natural-looking full and partial dentures, and gentle extractions when a tooth truly cannot be saved, always with sedation options discussed up front.

(05)  Emergency care

Same-day relief

Toothache, chipped tooth, or swelling? We reserve same-day slots so you are never left in pain. Call us at (281) 204-8100 as early as you can.

(06)  Technology

Modern tools, gentler visits

Advanced diagnostics catch problems earlier, when treatment is less invasive and less expensive.

Digital X-rays

Clearer images, less waiting, and far less radiation than traditional film X-rays.

Intraoral camera

A camera the size of a dental mirror shows you everything we see on a monitor: cracked teeth, plaque deposits, and cavities next to fillings, so you can make informed decisions.

CariVu cavity detection

Transillumination technology reveals cavities and cracks with no additional radiation, used alongside X-rays for an accurate, conservative diagnosis.

DIAGNOdent laser screening

A quick, comfortable laser scan finds the smallest areas of decay hiding in the grooves of your teeth, before they become larger problems.

3D scanning

Trios digital impressions and cone-beam CT imaging replace goopy molds and guesswork with precise digital models.
