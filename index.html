(05)  Good to know

Questions, answered

Everything you need before your first visit to Mill Forest Dental Group.

Are you accepting new patients?
Yes, we happily welcome new patients of all ages across the Clear Lake Area and surrounding communities. New‑patient exams often have same‑week availability, and you can book online or by phone in a couple of minutes.
Do you offer emergency dental appointments?
We reserve same‑day slots for dental emergencies such as toothaches, broken or knocked‑out teeth, lost fillings, and swelling. Call as early as you can and we'll get you seen quickly and comfortably.
Which insurance plans do you accept?
We accept most major PPO dental insurance plans and file your claims directly, so you only pay any remaining balance. We also offer flexible payment options for patients paying out of pocket.
How much does a check‑up and cleaning cost?
Our fees follow standard guidelines for the greater Houston area. We'll always confirm your estimate and insurance coverage before starting, so there are never surprise charges.
What are your hours and where are you located?
You'll find us at 17240 Mill Forest Rd, Suite C, in the Clear Lake Area. We're open Monday to Friday from 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. with Saturdays available by appointment and evening slots for busy schedules.
