Meet the doctors

Andrew Augustine, DDS

Meet Andrew Augustine, DDS, one of five dentists caring for Clear Lake families at Mill Forest Dental Group.

Serving the hometown he grew up in

“I found my passion for dentistry at a young age when I lost my permanent front tooth. My dentists were able to help me recover my smile and boost my self-esteem, and I am excited to increase the confidence and health of my patients in the same way.”

Born in Webster, Dr. Augustine attended Clear Springs High School and played collegiate golf at Southwestern University, where he earned his degree in biochemistry. At UTHealth Houston School of Dentistry he found both his dream career and his fiancée Logan, also a dentist practicing in the greater Clear Lake area.

He is certified in nitrous sedation to help alleviate anxiety, and is always exploring new techniques and treatment options. Off the clock you will find him with his two dogs, traveling, or at the beach.

Education and credentials

  • Bachelor of Science in Biochemistry, Southwestern University
  • Doctorate of Dental Surgery, UTHealth Houston School of Dentistry
  • Certified in nitrous sedation
  • Member, Academy of Laser Dentistry, TDA, and ADA
