Meet the doctors

Kenny Hasson, DDS

Meet Kenny Hasson, DDS, one of five dentists caring for Clear Lake families at Mill Forest Dental Group.

Building relationships, one smile at a time

“Dentistry allows me to live out my two greatest passions: building strong relationships within my community and caring for the people in it. I strive to create a comfortable environment for all patients and be a provider my patients can trust.”

Dr. Hasson keeps up with rapidly evolving dental technology through continuing education courses, journals, and video lectures, always looking for ways to make care higher quality and more comfortable.

He grew up in Deer Park and lives in the Clear Lake area with his two miniature dachshunds, Finn and Winston. He enjoys exercising, time with friends and family, and keeping up with all things sports.

Education and credentials

  • Bachelor of Science in Biology, University of Texas at San Antonio
  • Doctor of Dental Surgery, UTHealth Houston School of Dentistry
  • Member, Texas Dental Association and ADA
