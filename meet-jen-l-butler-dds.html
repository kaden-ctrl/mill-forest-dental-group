Meet the doctors

Jen L. Butler, DDS

Meet Jen L. Butler, DDS, one of five dentists caring for Clear Lake families at Mill Forest Dental Group.

Loves seeing families grow up with us

“I cannot express enough how much I love my career in dentistry. I feel honored that people choose me to take care of their oral health and make their smiles beautiful. After many years of practice, I have seen so many families grow, and grow up!”

Dr. Butler meets monthly with a study club of general dentists and specialists to review complex cases, and trains yearly through hands-on Spear workshops. She has completed Core I and II at the Las Vegas Institute for Advanced Dental Studies, and continues learning through the Greater Houston Dental Society and Ninth District Dental Society.

Born in Houston and raised around Austin, she met her husband Dan, an aerospace engineer for a NASA contractor, in dental school, and they settled in League City in 2005. They have three rambunctious boys, plus a Schnoodle named Snap and a bunny named Rosie.

Education and credentials

  • Bachelor of Arts in Microbiology, University of Texas at Austin
  • Doctorate of Dental Surgery, UTHealth Houston School of Dentistry
  • Fellow, Academy of General Dentistry
  • MBA in progress, University of Houston-Clear Lake
