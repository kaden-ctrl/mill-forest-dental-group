Meet the doctors

Jennifer H. Hermstein, DDS

Meet Jennifer H. Hermstein, DDS, one of five dentists caring for Clear Lake families at Mill Forest Dental Group.

Your neighbor at the store and the soccer game

“I love that our patients share their lives with us. I especially think it's great that the kids ask when they can come back again! Living in the same community where I practice, I see my patients at the store or at soccer games, and that really makes a difference in building trusting relationships.”

Dr. Hermstein is working toward Mastership in the Academy of General Dentistry, which requires more than double the annual continuing education of any other affiliation, and is a member of a local Spear study club.

A native of San Antonio, she has lived in League City since 2002 with her husband Scott and their three sons, Logan, Preston, and Grant. She enjoys reading, sewing, crafting, and photography, and volunteers with the Spanish dual-language program at Stewart Elementary.

Education and credentials

  • Bachelor of Arts, University of Texas at Austin
  • Doctorate of Dental Surgery, UT Health Science Center at San Antonio
  • Fellow, Academy of General Dentistry
